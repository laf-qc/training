# Independent audit brief — La Fromagerie Food Hygiene & Safety training

Please audit the complete static training course in this repository. Do not edit it during the first review. Produce a prioritised findings report with exact file/slide references and suggested corrections.

## Intended outcome

- Internal CPD food-hygiene training intended to be comparable in scope and learner understanding to common UK Level 2 courses.
- It is **not** currently an accredited Level 2 qualification.
- Audience: La Fromagerie colleagues in cheese rooms, cafe/restaurants and shops across three stores.
- Delivery: static GitHub Pages presentation with browser-local learner progress, 30-question assessment, 80% pass mark and internal certificate.

## Audit areas

1. **Food-safety accuracy:** Check every instruction, definition, temperature, time, exclusion period, allergen statement and example against current authoritative UK sources—preferably the Food Standards Agency, legislation.gov.uk and HSE.
2. **Level 2-equivalent coverage:** Compare the learning outcomes, depth and assessment coverage with a reputable Level 2 food safety specification. Identify missing or disproportionately treated topics.
3. **La Fromagerie context:** Flag statements that require confirmation against company procedures, especially cheese display/tempering, shelf lives, delivery limits, cleaning products, colour coding, escalation routes and record forms.
4. **Assessment validity:** Review all 30 questions for ambiguity, cueing, factual accuracy, coverage balance and answer-key correctness. Flag questions that are too easy, duplicated or do not test application.
5. **Learning design:** Review pacing, cognitive load, plain-English quality, scenario usefulness, module timing and whether recap/check content supports retention.
6. **Accessibility and UX:** Check keyboard operation, focus order, zoom/mobile behaviour, contrast, form labelling, screen-reader semantics, print/download behaviour and reduced-motion handling.
7. **Privacy and security:** Assess the access phrase, browser-local named profiles, shared-device risks, certificate integrity and suitability for an official training record. Distinguish pilot risks from production blockers.
8. **Claims and certificate wording:** Check that the site never implies external accreditation and that “intended to be equivalent to a Level 2 course” is appropriately qualified.

## Required report format

- Executive summary
- Critical blockers
- High-, medium- and low-priority findings
- Factual corrections with authoritative links
- Missing-content matrix by module
- Assessment question review table
- Accessibility/technical findings
- Recommended pre-launch checklist

For each finding include: severity, file/slide, current wording or behaviour, why it matters, recommended change and source/evidence.
