# Level 2 subject-coverage plan

## Purpose

Close the remaining gaps against common UK Level 2 food-safety subject matter while keeping the course concise and specific to La Fromagerie. This is an internal training course, not an accredited or regulated qualification.

## Planned additions

| Area | Content to add | Format | Assessment additions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food law and enforcement | Food Safety Act 1990, due diligence, Hygiene Improvement Notices, emergency prohibition, prosecution and food-hygiene ratings | Two short cards and one manager scenario | 5 banked questions |
| Pathogens and sources | Typical sources of Salmonella, Campylobacter, harmful E. coli, norovirus and Listeria | One route-matching card | 5 banked questions |
| Freezing and defrosting | Frozen storage target, safe freezing before use-by, labelling, controlled defrosting and preventing drip contamination | Two cards and one scenario | 6 banked questions |
| Spoilage | Harmful microorganisms versus spoilage; recognising abnormal mould, texture, odour, packaging and damage without treating sensory checks as proof of safety | One cheese-led card | 4 banked questions |
| Premises and equipment | Hygienic workflow, washable surfaces, maintenance, ventilation, handwash facilities and equipment that can be cleaned effectively | Two visual cards | 5 banked questions |
| Pest prevention | Rats, mice, flies, cockroaches, birds and stored-product insects; proofing, deliveries, waste and reporting | Two cards and a store-spotting activity | 5 banked questions |
| Allergen precautionary labels | Meaning and limits of “may contain”; no improvised free-from claims | One short card | 4 banked questions |
| Listeria application | Vulnerable customers, chilled growth, date control and safe pregnancy/raw-milk conversations | Existing dedicated card plus an interactive scenario | 5 banked questions |

## Delivery rules

- Target 13 additional presentation cards, not 20 dense reading slides.
- Make at least six additions interactive or scenario-led.
- Use literal rule-first headings and define technical terms at first use.
- Add department examples only where the rule differs or the context helps application.
- Validate legal and scientific statements against current FSA, GOV.UK, HSE or legislation sources before release.
- Obtain competent-person approval and record the reviewer, date and course version.

## Assessment plan

- Current upgrade: 40 reviewed four-option questions, with 30 drawn by module and choices shuffled per attempt.
- Production target: at least 75 reviewed questions after the coverage additions above.
- Maintain at least half of every draw as applied scenarios.
- Balance plausible option length and avoid absolute or humorous giveaway answers.
- Store a rationale, source, module/objective and review date for every production item.
- Give topic-level revision guidance after a failed attempt and require a manager conversation before a third attempt.

## Completion sequence

1. Add law/enforcement and premises/equipment.
2. Add freezing/defrosting and spoilage.
3. Add pathogen sources, pests and precautionary allergen labels.
4. Add the matching question-bank items and topic-level remediation.
5. Conduct competent-person review.
6. Pilot with staff from the cheese room, cafe/restaurant and shop.
7. Revise content and publish the measured completion time.
