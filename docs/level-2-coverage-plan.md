# Level 2 subject-coverage plan

## Purpose

Close the remaining gaps against common UK Level 2 food-safety subject matter while keeping the course concise and specific to La Fromagerie. This is an internal training course, not an accredited or regulated qualification.

## Delivery rules

- Prefer enriching an existing card where the subject has a natural home; avoid thin cards containing only one or two sentences.
- Use choose-first, tap-to-reveal interactions selectively on scenarios where committing to a decision improves learning.
- Use literal rule-first headings and define technical terms at first use.
- Add department examples only where the rule differs or the context helps application.
- Validate legal and scientific statements against current FSA, GOV.UK, HSE or legislation sources before release.
- Obtain competent-person approval and record the reviewer, date and course version.

## Assessment plan

- Current upgrade: 40 reviewed four-option questions, with 30 drawn by module and choices shuffled per attempt.
- Maintain at least half of every draw as applied scenarios.
- Balance plausible option length and avoid absolute or humorous giveaway answers.
- Store a rationale, source, module/objective and review date for every production item.
- The present 40-item bank and balanced 30-question draw are the agreed scope for this release.

## Completion sequence

1. Conduct competent-person review.
2. Complete user testing with staff from the cheese room, cafe/restaurant and shop.
3. Revise content and publish the measured completion time.
